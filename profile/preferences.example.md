# Job Search Preferences (example)

Copy this file to `profile/preferences.md` (gitignored) and edit it. It seeds the notes
field of your saved preferences the first time the app runs; after you save preferences
in the UI, the file is no longer read. The CV stays the source of truth for experience
and skills.

## Search objective

Find a small number of well-matched permanent roles. Prioritize role fit and credible
evidence from the CV over application volume.

## Role priorities

1. Your first-choice role family, e.g. "Machine Learning Engineer".
2. A close second, e.g. "Data Engineer with production ownership".
3. Other roles whose responsibilities strongly match the evidence in the CV.

Titles are signals, not hard requirements. Score the actual responsibilities and
required experience rather than the title alone.

## Location and work model

- Based in <city, country>.
- Include roles located in <country> and roles that can be done remotely from <region>.
- Prefer remote or hybrid roles near <city>.
- Do not include roles that require relocation outside <country>.

## Language

- List your languages and levels, e.g. English: C1. German: B1.
- Reject roles that require working proficiency above your level.
- Keep roles where a language is only preferred or optional, but surface it as a gap.

## Employment and seniority

- Permanent employment only; exclude freelance, contract, internship and thesis roles.
- Target <mid-level through senior> individual-contributor roles.
- Exclude director, head-of and executive roles unless the work is hands-on.
- State whether visa sponsorship is needed.

## Company preferences

- Optional: prefer startups, scale-ups or large employers when fit is otherwise equal.
- Company size is a scoring preference, not a hard filter.

## Eligibility and hard exclusions

Reject roles that:

- require a citizenship, clearance or eligibility condition you cannot meet;
- require relocation outside <country>;
- are not permanent employment; or
- are clearly outside your seniority range.

If eligibility wording is ambiguous, mark the job for manual review instead of
silently rejecting it.

## Scoring guidance

Weight demonstrated responsibility match more heavily than keyword overlap. List the
strongest positive signals for you, e.g. customer-facing delivery, production ownership,
or a specific stack. Penalize material experience gaps and location uncertainty. Never
compensate for a hard-filter failure with a high match score.

## Tracking and notifications

- Keep every discovered job and its history in the local database.
- Create or update a Notion entry only after you explicitly mark a job as applied.
- Surface newly discovered high-scoring jobs in a concise digest; skip empty digests.
